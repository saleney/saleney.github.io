# Salene’s World quality checklist

Use this before calling a new room, interaction, or page complete.

- Check a narrow phone and a desktop viewport.
- Confirm every interactive element is comfortably tappable and keyboard reachable.
- Make sure no content is clipped or creates horizontal page overflow.
- Check that modals, placards, and overlays close with Escape and fit within the viewport.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and keep motion optional.
- Confirm public edition content is intentionally public.
- Reuse existing visual patterns before inventing a new one.
- Keep the experience warm, curious, handmade, and easy to understand.
